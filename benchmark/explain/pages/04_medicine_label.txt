Paracetamol 500 mg tablets
Adults and children over 12 years: take 1 to 2 tablets every 4 to 6 hours as needed.
Do not take more than 8 tablets in 24 hours.
Do not take with other medicines that contain paracetamol.
Keep out of the reach of children. Store below 30 degrees C.
Expiry date: 08/2027. Batch: PCM2291.
