Photosynthesis
Green plants make their own food in the leaves. Chlorophyll traps light energy from the sun.
The plant takes in carbon dioxide from the air and water from the soil.
Glucose is made, and oxygen is given out.
The equation is: carbon dioxide + water -> glucose + oxygen, using light energy and chlorophyll.
In an experiment, a leaf kept in the dark for 48 hours gave a negative starch test.
