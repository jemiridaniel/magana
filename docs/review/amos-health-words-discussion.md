# Words to discuss with Dr. Amos (19 Nov 2026)

His additions of 17 Nov 2026 are all in use, unchanged. These words stop harmless school sentences. The suggestions are the developer's, for him to decide. Measured in docs/FINDINGS.md (19 Nov 2026).

What the suggestions mean:
- **strong**: one is enough to stop the page ("Please ask your teacher").
- **ordinary**: two different ones stop the page.
- **post-check only**: the page isn't stopped, but an explanation that adds the word is not spoken.
- **restricted**: counts only next to a number or a dose form ("1 tab every morning", "calcium 500 mg").
