# Recording Magana's phrases

Thank you for lending Magana your voice. Blind and low-vision people will hear these recordings every day, so a clear, warm, natural voice matters more than a perfect one.

## What to record

- Open **magana-phrases.xlsx** (in this folder) and choose your language's sheet.
- Each row is one phrase. Record the **Text to record** exactly as written, once per file.
- If a text reads wrong in your language, record what sounds right and write the change in **Notes**. We will update the app's text to match.
- **Numbers are never recorded.** The phone says them exactly as printed.
- **Health template parts** are the words between the numbers. Record each part on its own, as you would say it in the sentence.

## Where and how

- **A quiet room.** Close the windows. Turn off fans and the TV. Soft things (curtains, a bed, clothes) stop echo. A wardrobe full of clothes is a good studio.
- **Your phone's voice recorder:** Voice Memos on iPhone, Recorder or Voice Recorder on Android. Use the best quality setting.
- Hold the phone about **one hand's width (15–20 cm)** from your mouth, a little to the side, so your breath doesn't hit it.
- Speak at your **normal speed**, a little slower than chatting, as if reading to someone who is listening carefully. Keep the same distance and loudness for every phrase.
- Leave **one second of silence** before and after each phrase.
- Plain voice: friendly, not acting, no music.
- Drink water. Rest every 20 minutes. About 50 phrases an hour is a good pace.

## File names

- Name each file exactly as in the **File name** column, for example `ha-homeTitle.m4a` or `yo-tpl-medicine_label-take_times_a_day-1.m4a`.
- Any of these is fine: `.m4a`, `.mp3`, `.wav`. Only the name before the dot matters.
- If you record a phrase again, keep only the best take with that name.
- Mark **Recorded?** as *yes* in the spreadsheet for each file you keep.

## Sending them

- Put all the files in one folder named with your language and your name, for example `hausa-aisha`. Zip it if you can.
- Send it the way we agreed (shared drive link or WhatsApp document), together with the spreadsheet.
- Tell us your name as you'd like it credited, or if you'd rather not be named.

## What happens next

We check each file, even out the loudness, trim the silence and put it in the app (`scripts/import_clips.py`). Where a phrase has no recording yet, the phone's own voice says it, as now. Hausa, Yorùbá and Igbo are never read with an English voice.
