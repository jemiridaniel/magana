# Recording pack for Dr. Amos: Magana's English voice

Thank you for lending Magana your voice, Dr. Amos. This is the first English voice the app
has had — people using Magana in English have only heard the phone's own voice until now.

## What to record

- Open **priority-phrases.docx** or **priority-phrases.xlsx** (in this folder, next to this
  guide). It's 47 phrases, in six groups: the tour opening, the home screen, taking a photo,
  the reader's controls, what Magana says when something is unclear, and health-worker/medicine
  lines.
- Record each line **exactly as written**, once per file, in the order given.
- **Numbers are never recorded** — the phone always reads them aloud itself, exactly as
  printed.
- **The last group (marked HEALTH) needs word-for-word accuracy.** These are the lines Magana
  says about medicine and about talking to a health worker — things like *"This could be
  serious. Please go to the nearest clinic now, or call 112."* Please read these exactly as
  written, with no rephrasing, even if a different phrasing would sound just as natural to you.
  Everywhere else in the list, if a line genuinely reads awkwardly out loud, say what sounds
  right and note what you changed — we'll check it before using it.

## Added later

- **9 Nov 2026: one new phrase.** "Please wait, I'm still reading." (`en-stillReadingPleaseWait.m4a`,

## Where and how

- **A quiet room.** Close the windows, turn off fans and the TV. Soft things around you
  (curtains, a wardrobe full of clothes, a made bed) cut down echo.
- **Your phone's own voice recorder** — Voice Memos on iPhone, or Recorder/Voice Recorder on
  Android. Best quality setting if there's a choice.
- Hold the phone about **one hand's width away (15–20 cm) from your mouth**, slightly to the
  side so your breath doesn't hit the microphone directly.
- Speak at your **normal, natural pace** — a little slower than conversation, as you would
  reading aloud to someone listening carefully. Keep the same distance and loudness for every
  line, including the HEALTH ones: no need to put on a different "serious" voice for those,
  just read them accurately.
- Leave a **short pause (about a second) of silence before and after each phrase** — that gap
  is what lets us trim each recording apart automatically.
- One take per line is fine; if you redo one, just keep the best take under that same file name.

## File names

- Each phrase's file name is given in the **File name** column of the spreadsheet (for example
  `en-pictureTaken.m4a`). Name your recording exactly that way.
- Any of `.m4a`, `.mp3` or `.wav` is fine — only the name before the dot matters.
- About 45–50 phrases is roughly 20–30 minutes of actual recording at a natural pace — comfortable
  in one sitting, or split across two if you prefer.

## Sending them

- Put all the files in one folder (e.g. `magana-english-amos`) and, if you can, zip it.
- Send it **as a Google Drive folder link, or as an email attachment to support@magana.ng.**
- **Please don't send them as WhatsApp voice notes** (the microphone icon) — WhatsApp compresses
  those heavily and we lose quality we can't get back. If WhatsApp is easiest for you, send the
  files as a **document/attachment** (the paperclip, not the microphone) instead — that keeps
  the original file untouched.
- Let us know how you'd like to be credited (e.g. "Voice: Dr. Oluba Amos") or if you'd rather
  not be named.

## What happens next

We check every file against its phrase ID, even out the loudness, trim the silence at each end,
and check the HEALTH lines specifically for an exact word-for-word match before anything goes
into the app (`scripts/import_clips.py`, `scripts/check_health_phrases.py`). If anything needs a
retake, we'll tell you exactly which line and why, rather than ask you to redo the whole set.
