Magana for Android, test version 0.1.0+6 (26 October 2026). For the team's testers.

**Install:** on your phone, open https://magana.ng/download/android, then follow the 3 steps at https://magana.ng/android/. Android 7 or newer, about 34 MB. Install it over an older version; your settings stay.

**What's new**
- Pages in two columns are read left column first, then right. Say "next column" to jump.
- Hausa, Yorùbá and Igbo pages: one warning that special letters may be missed, and "Spell word" for words to check.
- No internet? Basic reading now works on the phone. The full reading follows when you are back online.
- The camera tells you to tilt the phone, and waits for a sharp picture.
- After a page, Magana may ask once a day: "Did you read this page on your own?"
- "Report a problem" in the app sends your feedback straight to the team.
- Explain stays off for now. When it is on, it only speaks answers whose numbers it can check.

**What to try:** https://magana.ng/test/

This is a test version: some things will go wrong. Please tell us with "Report a problem".
