Magana for Android, test version 0.1.0+7 (27 October 2026). For the team's testers.

**Install:** on your phone, open https://magana.ng/download/android, then follow the 3 steps at https://magana.ng/android/. Install it over the older version; your settings stay.

**What's fixed**
- Small things like cards, receipts, labels and boarding passes: the camera now takes the picture when it sees text, even without the edges of a whole page. If you hold steady on text for 6 seconds, it takes the picture anyway.
- "Move closer" is now only said when the text really is too small.
- Faint print, such as thermal receipts and boarding passes, is darkened before reading.
- A torn or cut edge: Magana reads what is there and tells you "Some words at the left edge are cut off."
- Fewer false "part of this line is unclear" warnings.

**What to try:** https://magana.ng/test/ — and please try a small card and a receipt.

This is a test version. Please tell us about anything wrong with "Report a problem".
