| `NotoSans-*.ttf` | Noto Sans, © The Noto Project Authors (subset) | SIL OFL 1.1, `OFL-NotoSans.txt` |

Atkinson Hyperlegible is the app font. Noto Sans is only the fallback for letters Atkinson lacks: Hausa ɓ ɗ ƙ ƴ, Yorùbá and Igbo ẹ ọ ṣ ị ụ ǹ, combining tone marks, and ₦.

The Noto files are static Regular (400) and Bold (700) instances of the variable font `NotoSans[wdth,wght].ttf` from github.com/google/fonts (ofl/notosans), subset with fontTools to Latin, Latin-1, Latin Extended-A/B, IPA, spacing modifiers, combining diacritics, Latin Extended Additional, general punctuation and currency symbols (U+0020-007E, U+00A0-02FF, U+0300-036F, U+1E00-1EFF, U+2000-206F, U+20A0-20CF, U+2122, U+2190-2193, U+2212).
